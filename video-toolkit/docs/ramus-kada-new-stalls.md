# Ramu's Kada — new stalls for the Orthognathic Surgery module

The same rules as the existing sketch: one character per surgeon, the **name is a pun**, and the **absurd action is the surgical feature**.
Style for every image prompt: *hand-drawn Kerala village-shop illustration, warm watercolour and ink, expressive cartoon characters, labelled with chalk signs, same style as "Ramu's Kada — Provisions for Better Faces".*

---

## Existing stalls, decoded (used in the explainer)

| Stall | Character → surgeon | Absurd action = feature |
|---|---|---|
| 1849* | **HULLIGAN** saws, rope round his neck → **Hullihen** | rope = burn-scar contracture released; first mandibular osteotomy (anterior alveolar segment) |
| 1907 | **BLAIR THE LIAR** → **Blair** | one plank cut straight across = horizontal ramus osteotomy (external); small contact → relapse |
| 1942 | Cat in a **BOOT** with a **CHART** → **Shoe-chart = Schuchardt** | climbs a STEP = step cut; first intraoral approach to the ramus |
| 1957 | **HUGO + TRAUMA** carry a split **GEYSER** → **Obwegeser & Trauner** | geyser split down the middle = sagittal split |
| 1961 | **DAL** + pipe that bends **down** → **Dal Pont** | bend = vertical buccal cut, 1st–2nd molar |
| 1968* | **HEN SUCKS** a **short** straw → **Hunsuck** | short straw = short medial cut, just behind the lingula |
| 1977 | **ABKARI** slicing carefully → **Epker** | careful knife = minimal stripping of masseter & medial pterygoid |

\* Dates differ between sources: Hullihen 1846–1849, Hunsuck 1967/1968.

## New stalls to draw

### 1 · Vertical ramus osteotomy — "COLD-WELL & LETTER-MAN" (1954)
- **Facts:** Caldwell & Letterman 1954, extraoral vertical ramus osteotomy for prognathism. Cut from the sigmoid notch to the angle, **behind the mandibular foramen**. Set-back only; low IAN injury; usually needs IMF; watch for condylar sag.
- **Scene:** A postman (LETTER-MAN, satchel labelled "LETTERS") drops a letter **straight down** a frosty well with a sign "COLD WELL". The rope hangs perfectly vertical. He walks **backwards** away from the well (set-back only). His wrists are tied with a ribbon tag "6 WEEKS" (IMF).
- **Prompt:** `A Kerala village shop stall: a cheerful postman labelled "LETTER-MAN" dropping an envelope straight down a frosty stone well signed "COLD WELL", a perfectly vertical rope, the postman walking backwards, his wrists tied with a ribbon tag "6 WEEKS", hand-drawn watercolour and ink, expressive cartoon style, chalk sign "1954"`

### 2 · First intraoral VRO — "WIN-STANLEY" (1968)
- **Facts:** Winstanley 1968 reported the first **intraoral** vertical ramus osteotomy (IVRO).
- **Scene:** A man lifts a hockey **Stanley Cup** (he **won** it) while standing *inside* a giant open-mouth doorway (intra-oral), the same vertical rope from the well beside him.
- **Prompt:** `A man labelled "STANLEY" triumphantly lifting a silver Stanley Cup trophy while standing inside a giant open-mouth-shaped doorway of a Kerala shop, a vertical rope hanging beside him, sign "1968 — INSIDE THE MOUTH", hand-drawn watercolour and ink cartoon`

### 3 · Inverted-L osteotomy — "RAMU'S UPSIDE-DOWN WALKING STICK"
- **Facts:** A horizontal cut above the lingula plus a vertical cut down to the lower border; keeps the IAN out of the cut. A similar design was described by **Wassmund (1927)**.
- **Scene:** Ramu's walking stick hangs **upside-down** on a hook, so its handle makes the "Γ" (inverted L). Under it, a laundry **mound** is being **washed** in a basin ("WASH-MOUND" = Wassmund).
- **Prompt:** `A wooden walking stick hanging upside-down on a hook forming an inverted L shape, below it a woman washing a big mound of laundry in a basin labelled "WASH-MOUND 1927", Kerala shop wall, hand-drawn watercolour and ink cartoon`

### 4 · Maxilla — "THE FORT, FIRST FLOOR" (Le Fort I)
- **Facts:** Named after René Le Fort's 1901 fracture lines. Wassmund 1927 (first Le Fort I-level osteotomy for a deformity) → Axhausen 1934 → Obwegeser 1965 (complete mobilisation) → Bell 1975 (biological basis / blood supply).
- **Scene:** A toy **fort** on Ramu's counter with its **ground floor sliding out horizontally** like a drawer (Le Fort I = horizontal cut above the teeth). Around it: the WASH-MOUND lady (Wassmund); a man with an **AXE** in a little **house** ("AX-HAUSEN"); Hugo pulling the whole floor completely free (full mobilisation); and a temple **BELL** ringing with a tiny red blood-drop clapper (Bell = blood supply).
- **Prompt:** `A toy fort on a shop counter whose ground floor slides out horizontally like a drawer, a man with an axe in a small house labelled "AX-HAUSEN", a strongman labelled "HUGO" pulling the floor completely free, a brass temple bell with a red drop-shaped clapper labelled "BELL", hand-drawn watercolour Kerala shop style`

### 5 · Genioplasty — "THE GENIE'S CHIN"
- **Facts:** Intraoral osseous genioplasty: Trauner & Obwegeser, 1957.
- **Scene:** A lamp **genie** with a long chin. Hugo and Trauma slide the genie's chin **forward** like a sliding shelf.
- **Prompt:** `A friendly blue genie rising from a brass lamp, two shopkeepers labelled "HUGO" and "TRAUMA" sliding the genie's chin forward like a drawer, sign "1957 INSIDE THE MOUTH", hand-drawn watercolour Kerala shop style`

### 6 · Complications corner — "DON'T CUT THE CABLE"
- **Facts:** BSSO complications: IAN neurosensory disturbance (most common), bad split, condylar sag, relapse, bleeding.
- **Scene:** A live **electric cable** (IAN) runs behind the counter with a sign "NERVE — DON'T CUT". A coconut cracked the wrong way (bad split). A sagging hammock tied to the condyle-shaped post (condylar sag). A rubber band snapping back (relapse).
- **Prompt:** `Behind a Kerala shop counter: a yellow electric cable with a warning sign "NERVE — DON'T CUT", a coconut cracked the wrong way labelled "BAD SPLIT", a sagging hammock labelled "SAG", a rubber band snapping back labelled "RELAPSE", hand-drawn watercolour and ink cartoon`
