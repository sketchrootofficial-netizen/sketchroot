# Notes and sources

## Style decisions
- **Target style:** a voice-led memory-palace video. One scene starts empty, each symbol is painted in (pencil → flat colour → shading) when the narrator mentions it, the camera drifts to the element being discussed, and arrows, tags and summary boxes stay sparse. The text-heavy explainer in `ramus/` is the earlier, text-only version.
- **Reels:** the hook lands in the first second, captions reveal word by word on the music's beats, and the end card asks a comment question ("Which subject is the hardest for you to recall?").
- **Brand:** ink `#272525`, amber `#F6B31E`, paper `#FCFBF7`. Karla for text, Caveat (handwritten) for accents.

## Content checks still open (have an OMFS colleague verify before release)
- Disputed dates: Hullihen 1846–1849; Obwegeser's first sagittal-split description 1953–55 (paper with Trauner 1957); Hunsuck 1967 or 1968.
- Hullihen's burn-scar contracture detail; Blair 1907 external horizontal ramus osteotomy; Schuchardt 1942 as the first intraoral ramus approach plus the step modification.
- Left out because unconfirmed: the "C-osteotomy" (Caldwell et al. 1968) attribution, and Hofer 1942 as the first genioplasty.
