**11:56** `[CAM: pgi]` `[ADD: pgi]`

**12:06** `[CAM: pgi]`

**12:22** `[CAM: pgi]`

Keep in mind:
- Arg-gingipains (RgpA, RgpB) and Lys-gingipain (Kgp)

- Black pigment on blood agar = stored heme

**12:45** `[CAM: pgi]`
