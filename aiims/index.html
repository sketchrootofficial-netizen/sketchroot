AIIMS entrance for dental graduates

Dental graduates reach AIIMS for postgraduate study in two main ways: MDS seats filled through INI CET, and research degrees through the AIIMS PhD entrance examination. AIIMS no longer holds a separate postgraduate entrance test, so MDS admission runs through INI CET, conducted by AIIMS New Delhi.

MDS at AIIMS through INI CET

MDS seats at AIIMS are filled through INI CET, the examination AIIMS New Delhi conducts for the Institutes of National Importance. It is separate from NEET MDS. Read our guide to INI CET for dental graduates for the pattern, the marking scheme and how to prepare.

The AIIMS PhD entrance

AIIMS also admits research scholars through its PhD entrance examination, which is notified separately from INI CET. Dr. Jishnu Mohan, founder of SketchRoot, secured All India Rank 1 in the AIIMS New Delhi PhD entrance examination in 2025 in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Syllabus, eligibility and dates change with each notification, so treat the official AIIMS notice as your only source for them.

General advice that holds for any research entrance: build fluency in reading journal papers in your speciality, revise basic biostatistics and study design, and practise explaining a study in a few sentences.

What AIIMS style questions look like

Published guides describe AIIMS style papers as scenario driven, image rich and less tolerant of one line memorisation. The practical response is to study each topic as a connected story: presentation, investigation, diagnosis, management and complications in one frame. That is how SketchRoot scenes are drawn.

A checklist before you apply

  • Read the current information bulletin on aiimsexams.ac.in from start to finish.
  • Check eligibility, including internship completion rules and registration requirements.
  • Decide whether you are targeting the January or the July session.
  • Plan your preparation window backward from the examination date.
  • Keep official notices in one folder and ignore forwarded messages.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an MDS seat at AIIMS?

MDS seats at AIIMS are filled through INI CET, conducted by AIIMS New Delhi.

Does AIIMS have its own MDS entrance exam?

No. AIIMS admits MDS candidates through INI CET rather than through a separate entrance examination.

What is the AIIMS PhD entrance?

It is the examination AIIMS conducts to admit research scholars to its PhD programmes. Details are published in the official AIIMS notification for each cycle.

Who secured AIR 1 in the AIIMS PhD entrance 2025 in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery?
