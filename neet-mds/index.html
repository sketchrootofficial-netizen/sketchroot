NEET MDS preparation guide

NEET MDS is the national entrance examination for Master of Dental Surgery seats in India, conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). It is a computer based test of 240 multiple choice questions, four marks for each correct answer and one mark deducted for each wrong answer, so the paper is scored out of 960.

What NEET MDS tests

NEET MDS covers the whole BDS curriculum, so the paper rewards breadth first and depth second. A large share of questions ask you to recall a specific fact, a classification, a landmark or a drug, which is why revision speed and retention matter as much as understanding.

Your score decides your rank, and your rank decides which college and which speciality you can choose at counselling. NEET MDS scores are used for MDS admission across government, private, deemed and central institutions.

The Institutes of National Importance, including AIIMS, admit through a separate examination. See our guide to INI CET for dental graduates for how that paper differs.

Exam pattern and marking scheme

The paper has 240 questions and 960 marks. It is delivered in two timed parts, 75 minutes for the first and 105 minutes for the second. Because you cannot go back to the first part once its time is over, deciding where to spend time is part of the examination. Always read the current information bulletin on the NBEMS website, because details of the paper can change between years.

What negative marking means for guessing

With four marks for a correct answer and one mark lost for a wrong answer, a completely blind guess on a four option question has an expected value of plus 0.25 marks: a one in four chance of gaining 4 and a three in four chance of losing 1. Ruling out even one option lifts that to about plus 0.67. The lesson is not to guess wildly. It is to stop leaving questions blank when you can eliminate any option.

Subjects to cover

  • Preclinical: general anatomy including embryology and histology, general physiology, biochemistry, dental anatomy and histology, dental materials.
  • Paraclinical: general pathology and microbiology, pharmacology, oral pathology.
  • Clinical: oral medicine and radiology, oral and maxillofacial surgery, conservative dentistry and endodontics, prosthodontics, periodontology, orthodontics and dentofacial orthopaedics, pedodontics and preventive dentistry, public health dentistry, and the general medicine and general surgery taught in the BDS.

NBEMS publishes the syllabus and the distribution of questions in its information bulletin. We do not publish weightage figures, because unofficial numbers differ between sources and between years.

A preparation plan that fits the paper

  1. First pass. One standard textbook per subject and one MCQ source alongside it. Secure breadth before chasing depth.
  2. Topic wise practice. Attempt questions as each subject closes and keep an error log that records why each miss happened.
  3. Timed mocks. Practise full length papers in two parts, 75 and 105 minutes, to rehearse the real time pressure.
  4. Spaced revision. Revisit every subject on a schedule such as day 1, day 3, day 7, day 21 and day 60 instead of rereading in one block.

Previous year questions are the best map of what the exam repeats. Read how to use NEET MDS previous year questions before you spend months on them.

Why visual memory suits NEET MDS

Dentistry is dense with classifications, sequences and relationships. Research on dual coding, the idea that information held as both words and images is easier to retrieve, and on spaced retrieval practice both support building revision around images you actively recall rather than pages you passively reread (Clark and Paivio, 1991; Roediger and Karpicke, 2006; Cepeda and colleagues, 2006).

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are in NEET MDS?

NEET MDS has 240 multiple choice questions, each with one correct option, scored out of 960 marks.

Is there negative marking in NEET MDS?

Yes. A correct answer earns 4 marks, a wrong answer loses 1 mark, and an unattempted question scores 0.

Who conducts NEET MDS?

The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) conducts NEET MDS as a computer based test once a year.

How long is the NEET MDS paper?

Three hours in total, in two timed parts of 75 and 105 minutes. A part cannot be revisited after its time ends.

Does NBEMS release the NEET MDS question paper?

No. NBEMS does not release official question papers. Papers circulating online are recalled by candidates and may contain errors.

What is the NEET MDS syllabus?

The BDS curriculum, covering preclinical, paraclinical and clinical subjects. The NBEMS information bulletin is the official reference.
