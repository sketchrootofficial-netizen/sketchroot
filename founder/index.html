Dr. Jishnu Mohan, founder of SketchRoot

Dr. Jishnu Mohan is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon and head and neck surgical oncologist, and the founder of SketchRoot. He secured All India Rank 1 in the AIIMS New Delhi PhD entrance examination in 2025 (Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery), All India Rank 37 in NEET MDS 2022 and All India Rank 91 in INI CET 2022, and completed his M.D.S. as a gold medalist and university subject topper.

About Dr. Jishnu Mohan

Dr. Mohan trained in oral and maxillofacial surgery and head and neck surgical oncology, and worked as a consultant in head and neck surgical oncology at Tata Memorial Hospital. His work spans clinical surgery and computational engineering, including patient specific craniofacial implant design using finite element analysis and additive manufacturing.

His professional practice and portfolio are at doctorj.in.

Why he built SketchRoot

Dental students are not short of information. They are drowning in it, and almost none of it is designed for the way human memory works. Linear text is a poor container for high volume, high recall material, and dentistry is exactly that.

SketchRoot turns each high yield topic into one illustrated scene that carries the classification, mechanism, features and management together, built for aspirants of NEET MDS, INI CET, INBDE and AIIMS. The platform is being built now, and early access is open.
